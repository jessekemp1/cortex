## Key Capabilities by 2030

### Predictive Planning: 6-12 Month Scenarios

**What It Does**:
- Plans 6-12 months ahead with high accuracy
- Multiple scenario bands (optimistic/likely/conservative)
- Confidence intervals, not false precision
- Continuous recalibration from reality

**How It Works**:
- Virtual twin with learned models
- Historical pattern recognition
- External factor integration
- Monte Carlo simulation

**Value**:
- Long-term strategic planning
- Risk assessment
- Resource allocation
- Opportunity evaluation

**Example**:
```
converx forecast "Ship Product X in 6 months"

SCENARIO BANDS (6-Month Horizon):
Optimistic: 4 months (90% confidence)
  Conditions: No major blockers, high momentum, team aligned
  Risks: Scope creep, external dependencies

Likely: 6 months (70% confidence)
  Conditions: Normal pace, 1-2 minor blockers, team stable
  Risks: Technical challenges, resource constraints

Conservative: 9 months (50% confidence)
  Conditions: Interruptions, multiple blockers, team changes
  Risks: Major technical issues, market changes
```
