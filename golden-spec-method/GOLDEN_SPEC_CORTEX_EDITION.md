# Golden Spec Method: The Research Edition

**Version**: 4.1 (Outcome-Focused)
**Date**: December 2025
**Mission**: To research and build the ideas, tools, and systems that expand human intelligence.

---

## 1. The Vision
**"We build tools that help you think better."**

Everything around us was once just an idea. The computer, the internet, the pencil. Someone had to **research** it, **understand** it, and **build** it.

Our mission is to research new ways to help humans think, create, and solve problems. We don't just write code; we build **systems** (like strategic intelligence tools or autonomous coding agents) that give people "superpowers" to solve the world's hardest challenges.

We believe in:
1.  **Truth-Seeking**: Seeing the world exactly as it is, so we can change it.
2.  **Maximum Potential**: Dreaming of the best possible future, then building the steps to get there.
3.  **Compound Wisdom**: Making sure everything we build helps us build the next thing even better.

---

## 2. How We Build (The Framework)

We use a 7-step process to turn wild ideas into real tools that change lives.

### Phase 1: Deep Research (The "Why")
Before we build, we explore. We ask "Why?" five times. We look at the history of the problem. We find the **truth**.
*   *Outcome*: We know exactly what problem we are solving and why it matters.

### Phase 2: Design the Dream (The "What")
We imagine the perfect version of the future. What if a computer could write its own code? What if a tool could predict your next best move? We define this "Dream State" clearly.
*   *Outcome*: A clear picture of success that everyone can understand.

### Phase 3: The Reality Check
We ask: "Is this actually possible?" and "Is this the most important thing to work on?" We make sure we aren't wasting time.
*   *Outcome*: Confidence that we are building the right thing.

### Phase 4: Creative Design (The "How")
This is where we invent. We combine our creative ideas with hard engineering. We sketch, we prototype, and we figure out how to make the dream real.
*   *Outcome*: A blueprint for a tool that works.

### Phase 5: Connecting the Dots
We make sure every part of our invention actually helps solve the problem. No wasted parts. Everything has a purpose.
*   *Outcome*: A system where every piece matters.

### Phase 6: Building the Plan
We break the big dream into small, easy steps. We decide who does what and when.
*   *Outcome*: A simple list of steps to follow.

### Phase 7: Testing & Learning
We build it, use it, and see if it works. Then we ask: "What did we learn?" so we can make the next version even better.
*   *Outcome*: A working tool and new knowledge for the next adventure.
