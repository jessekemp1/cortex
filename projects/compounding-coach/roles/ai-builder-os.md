# AI Builder OS

## Role Identity

Become a visible AI builder who learns publicly, ships small artifacts, and compounds reputation through useful experiments.

The goal is not to launch one giant perfect product. The goal is to become someone who ships useful work consistently.

## Artifact Ladder

1. Insight note
2. Prompt
3. Screenshot
4. Diagram
5. Short demo video
6. Tiny script
7. GitHub issue or roadmap note
8. Blog post
9. Repo update
10. Open-source tool
11. Productized workflow

## Weekly Shipping Rule

Never let a week end without one public or semi-public artifact.

## Not-Ready-Yet Decision Tree

When the thought appears: this is not ready, ask:

1. Can one person benefit from this today?
2. Can it ship as a lesson instead of a product?
3. Can it be labeled experimental?
4. Can scope remove the support burden?
5. What is the smallest honest version?

If one person can benefit, ship a small version.

## Evidence to Capture

- What shipped
- Who saw it
- What response came back
- What surprised me
- What became easier after sharing
- What fear or avoidance showed up
- What was learned about taste, skill, or direction

## Weekly Review Questions

- What did I publish or make visible?
- What did reality teach me?
- What feedback did I receive?
- What idea keeps recurring?
- What project is too big and needs to be split?
- What should ship next week?

## Anti-Patterns

- Building the cathedral before showing the brick
- Confusing support burden with usefulness
- Treating public work as performance instead of a lab notebook
- Letting private ambition become public invisibility
