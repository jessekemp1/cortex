# Databricks Leadership OS

## Role Identity

Become the Databricks leader who creates customer impact, makes strategic momentum visible, and helps account teams and executives see what is coming next.

The goal is not to look busy. The goal is to create confidence.

## Weekly Account Impact Signal

```text
Account:
Date:
What changed:
Measured or observed impact:
Why it matters:
Emerging risk or opportunity:
What is next:
Who needs to know:
Ask or recommendation:
```

## Evidence to Capture

- Customer decisions influenced
- Executive alignment created
- Risks discovered earlier
- Technical blockers removed
- Time saved
- Architecture decisions clarified
- Pipeline or expansion influence
- Internal team confidence improved
- Reusable assets created
- Strategic insight surfaced

## Visibility Rules

1. Share before something is perfect.
2. Make impact legible, not loud.
3. Tie technical progress to customer value.
4. Make the next move obvious.
5. Do not assume leaders can infer impact.

## Weekly Review Questions

- What did I make visible this week?
- Who now has more confidence because of my work?
- What customer or account risk did I reduce?
- What reusable insight did I create?
- What is the next strategic signal I should send?
- Where did I stay too invisible?

## Anti-Patterns

- Waiting until the work is big enough to summarize
- Assuming account teams will tell the story
- Treating visibility as ego rather than leadership hygiene
- Only communicating when asked
- Letting great work remain local to one conversation
