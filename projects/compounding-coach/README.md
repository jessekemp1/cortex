# Compounding Coach Project

A Cortex project to test whether small visible acts, captured consistently, compound into measurable growth across work, building, health, family, and relationships.

This is not a habit tracker. It is a compounding-intelligence experiment.

## Thesis

When a person captures evidence weekly, reflects with memory, ships small artifacts, and reviews patterns monthly, their judgment, visibility, confidence, and relationships improve faster than through isolated effort.

## Six-Month Objective

From July 2026 through December 2026, use Cortex to collect signals, detect patterns, and run weekly/monthly review loops that improve:

1. Leadership visibility
2. AI builder public shipping
3. Family and relationship presence
4. Health and nervous-system resilience
5. Personal identity alignment

## Core Loop

1. Monday: set intent
2. Tuesday: capture evidence
3. Thursday: capture evidence and identify one tiny shipment
4. Friday: weekly shipping review
5. Last Friday: monthly CEO review
6. Quarter end: compounding-pattern review

## Success Criteria

By the end of 6 months, the project should show evidence of:

- More visible work impact signals
- More public AI artifacts shipped
- Better feedback loops
- Repeated blind spots becoming visible sooner
- Avoidance patterns shrinking or becoming more conscious
- Stronger health consistency
- More grounded relational leadership
- A written proof trail showing that small acts compounded

## Folders

- `roles/` - operating systems for each life role
- `loops/` - recurring schedules and review prompts
- `schemas/` - JSONL schemas for evidence and reviews
- `prompts/` - reusable agent prompts
- `logs/` - human-readable weekly/monthly log templates
- `outputs/` - generated summaries and public artifacts

## Principle

Do not wait for a perfect system. Capture one useful signal and ship one small act of value. The system gets smarter only if it has evidence to learn from.
