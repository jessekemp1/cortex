## Key Concepts

### Decision Intelligence

**Definition**: The ability to make optimal decisions by combining human intuition with AI precision, with full predictive awareness of immediate actions, long-term trajectories, and cross-domain impacts.

**How Converx enables it**: Unifies context, predicts outcomes, models scenarios, provides cross-domain awareness.

### Risk/Opportunity Analysis

**Definition**: Hyper-predictive analysis that detects blockers, risks, and opportunities before they emerge.

**How Converx enables it**: Early-warning system that analyzes project activity, goals, and domain states to forecast risks/opportunities.

### Hybrid Local/Connected

**Definition**: Local-first architecture (data stays on your machine) with optional cloud/AI augmentation when beneficial.

**How Converx implements it**: Core logic and data are local, but can tap external models/APIs when you choose.

### Multi-Domain Awareness

**Definition**: Understanding how decisions cascade across work, finance, health, and learning domains.

**How Converx enables it**: Life Weather Map tracks state across domains and models cross-domain impacts.
