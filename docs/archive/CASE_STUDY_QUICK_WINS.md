# Cortex Case Study - Quick Wins

**TL;DR of the 30-day study**

---

## The Numbers

- **ROI**: 20x (1.5 hours invested → 31 hours saved)
- **Velocity**: +39% daily commits
- **Time savings**: 6 hours/week
- **Success rate**: 81% on followed recommendations
- **Context switching**: -80% time reduction

---

## The 5 Biggest Wins

### 1. VortexV2 Deployment (Week 1)
> Procrastinated validation for 7 days. Briefing guilt-tripped me into action.
> **Result**: Deployed to production, 15% accuracy improvement

### 2. Strategic Decision (Week 4)
> System showed Alpha Arena had 88% success probability vs VortexV2 at 50%.
> **Result**: Prevented wrong decision, unblocked entire roadmap

### 3. Cross-Project Learning (Week 3)
> Pattern detection linked VortexV2 risk logic → Alpha Arena position sizing.
> **Result**: Reused code, saved 2 hours design work

### 4. Drift Detection (Week 2)
> Alert: 45 uncommitted changes before context switch.
> **Result**: Prevented data loss

### 5. Learning Evolution (Month 1)
> System learned documentation = 33% success, validation = 100% success.
> **Result**: Stopped recommending docs, boosted validation tasks

## Example: The Alpha Arena Call

**Gut instinct**: Work on VortexV2 (fun, shiny, in my head)

**Cortex data**:
- Alpha Arena: 88% success probability, unblocks roadmap
- VortexV2: 50% success probability, can wait
- Documentation: 33% success probability, defer

**Decision**: Follow the data → Alpha Arena

**Outcome**: Completed in 1 day, found critical bug, unblocked roadmap

**Cost of wrong decision**: 1+ week delay, compounding blockers

**Value of right decision**: Priceless
