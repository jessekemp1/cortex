# Personal Decision Intelligence Platform Research

## Mission
Reverse-engineer the core value propositions of Palantir Gotham/Foundry and Databricks to architect a **Personal Decision Intelligence Platform** for solo builders and small AI teams.

## Target Audience
- Solo AI builders
- Small teams (2-5 people)
- NOT enterprise, government, or military

## Core Outcomes to Enable
1. **Thriving** - Sustainable growth and success
2. **Power** - Leverage and capability multiplication
3. **Safety** - Risk awareness and mitigation
4. **Autonomy** - Independence from gatekeepers
5. **Control** - Mastery over one's domain
6. **Prediction** - Foresight and pattern recognition
7. **Building/Innovating** - Creation velocity
8. **Surviving & Adapting** - Resilience and evolution

---

## Research Streams

### Stream 1: Palantir Gotham Deep Dive
**Focus**: Intelligence analysis patterns
- Link analysis and entity resolution
- Timeline reconstruction
- Pattern-of-life analysis
- Hypothesis generation
- Multi-source data fusion
- Graph-based reasoning

### Stream 2: Palantir Foundry Deep Dive
**Focus**: Data integration and workflows
- Ontology management (semantic layer)
- Data lineage and provenance
- Pipeline orchestration
- Code workbooks and transformations
- Operational dashboards
- Collaboration primitives

### Stream 3: Databricks Analysis
**Focus**: Unified analytics platform
- Lakehouse architecture
- Unity Catalog (governance)
- MLflow integration
- Delta Lake (versioned data)
- Notebook-first workflows
- Feature stores

### Stream 4: Solo Builder Needs Analysis
**Focus**: What individuals actually need
- Personal knowledge management
- Decision journaling
- Outcome tracking
- Resource constraints
- Privacy requirements
- Integration with AI assistants

### Stream 5: Competitive Landscape
**Focus**: Existing tools in the space
- Notion/Obsidian (PKM)
- Retool/Airplane (internal tools)
- Hex/Observable (analytics)
- LangChain/LlamaIndex (AI infra)
- Temporal/Prefect (orchestration)

---

## Research Questions

### Architecture
1. What is the minimal viable ontology for personal decision intelligence?
2. How do you scale data integration without a data team?
3. What's the right abstraction level for a solo builder?

### Capability Translation
1. What does "link analysis" look like for a solo builder's data?
2. How do you do "pattern of life" analysis on your own activities?
3. What's the personal equivalent of Foundry's operational dashboards?

### AI Integration
1. How do LLMs change the decision intelligence paradigm?
2. What should be automated vs. human-in-the-loop?
3. How do you build institutional memory as an individual?

### Economics
1. What's the cost structure that works for solo builders?
2. Build vs. buy vs. integrate decisions?
3. Data sovereignty and privacy trade-offs?
