# Converx Ramp-Up Plan: From First Use to Mastery

**Version**: 1.0  
**Date**: January 2025  
**Purpose**: Optimized path to bring Converx to maximum potential

---

## Overview

This plan provides a structured, week-by-week path from your first `converx next` command to full mastery of the system. Each phase builds on the previous, with clear success criteria and optimization checkpoints.

**Key Principle**: Start simple, add complexity only when value is proven. Converx should feel like it's helping from day one, not requiring weeks of setup.

---

## Week 1: Foundation (Discovery)

### Day 1-2: First Contact

**Goal**: Understand what Converx does and how it works

**Actions**:
1. Run `converx next` - See your first strategic recommendation
2. Run `converx status` - Understand your current state
3. Read the output carefully - What does it tell you?
4. Try `converx next PROJECT_NAME` - Filter to a specific project

**What to Look For**:
- Does the recommendation make sense?
- Is the current state accurate?
- What feels useful? What feels confusing?

**Success Criteria**: You understand what Converx is showing you, even if you don't act on it yet

---

### Day 3-4: Daily Check-In

**Goal**: Establish the habit of checking your strategic position

**Actions**:
1. Each morning: Run `converx next`
2. Each evening: Run `converx status`
3. Observe patterns: What changes day to day?
4. Note what's useful: What helps you make decisions?

**What to Look For**:
- Are recommendations consistent or changing?
- Does the current state reflect reality?
- Are you starting to trust the output?

**Success Criteria**: Converx is part of your daily routine, even if just for observation

---

### Day 5-7: Integration

**Goal**: Begin acting on recommendations

**Actions**:
1. Morning: Run `converx next`, pick one action to focus on
2. Track: Did you complete it? Was the estimate accurate?
3. Evening: Run `converx status`, reflect on the day
4. Adjust: What would make Converx more useful?

**What to Look For**:
- Are recommendations actionable?
- Are effort estimates reasonable?
- Is the rationale clear?

**Success Criteria**: You're using Converx to guide at least one decision per day

---

## Week 2-4: Integration (Habit Formation)

### Week 2: Morning Routine

**Goal**: Establish morning strategic check-in

**Actions**:
1. **Morning Ritual**: Before checking email/Slack, run `converx next`
2. **Strategic Focus**: Pick the top recommendation as your focus for the day
3. **Context Loading**: Use `converx next --with-context` when starting new work
4. **Project Filtering**: Use `converx next PROJECT` when deep in a project

**Optimization**:
- Set up a shell alias: `alias cx='converx'` for faster access
- Consider adding to your shell profile: `converx next` on terminal open
- Track: How often do you follow the recommendation?

**Success Criteria**: Morning check-in is automatic, recommendations are trusted

---

### Week 3: Evening Reflection

**Goal**: Add reflection to close the feedback loop

**Actions**:
1. **Evening Review**: Run `converx status` to see what changed
2. **Pattern Recognition**: What patterns do you notice?
3. **Calibration**: Were estimates accurate? Were priorities right?
4. **Adjustment**: Update ACTION_PLAN.md if goals have changed

**Optimization**:
- Create a reflection template: What did I plan? What did I do? What did I learn?
- Track prediction accuracy: Did Converx's estimates match reality?
- Note blockers: What prevented you from following recommendations?

**Success Criteria**: Evening reflection is routine, you're noticing patterns

---

### Week 4: Project-Specific Workflows

**Goal**: Use Converx for focused project work

**Actions**:
1. **Project Deep Dives**: Use `converx next PROJECT` when working on a project
2. **Context Switching**: Use `converx next` to decide which project to focus on
3. **Blocker Resolution**: Use recommendations to clear blockers
4. **Goal Alignment**: Ensure recommendations align with ACTION_PLAN.md goals

**Optimization**:
- Create project-specific workflows: When do you use global vs project-specific?
- Track cross-project insights: Does Converx help you see connections?
- Measure focus: Are you more focused when using Converx?

**Success Criteria**: Converx is integrated into your project workflow

## Month 2-3: Optimization (Pattern Recognition)

### Month 2: Prediction Accuracy

**Goal**: Begin tracking and improving prediction accuracy

**Actions**:
1. **Track Estimates**: Record Converx's effort estimates vs actual time
2. **Track Outcomes**: Did you complete recommended actions? Why or why not?
3. **Identify Biases**: Is Converx systematically optimistic or pessimistic?
4. **Calibrate Expectations**: Adjust your interpretation based on accuracy

**Metrics to Track**:
- Effort estimate accuracy (predicted vs actual hours)
- Recommendation completion rate (% of recommendations you followed)
- Priority accuracy (were high-priority items actually important?)
- Blocker prediction (did Converx identify blockers before you hit them?)

**Optimization**:
- Create a simple tracking system: Spreadsheet or note file
- Weekly review: What patterns are emerging?
- Adjust ACTION_PLAN.md: Ensure goals are well-structured for parsing

**Success Criteria**: You understand Converx's accuracy patterns and adjust accordingly

---

### Month 3: Personal Patterns

**Goal**: Identify your personal patterns and biases

**Actions**:
1. **Pattern Analysis**: What patterns does Converx reveal about you?
2. **Bias Recognition**: What are your systematic biases? (optimism, scope creep, etc.)
3. **Velocity Calibration**: What's your actual sustainable pace?
4. **Energy Patterns**: When are you most effective? Does Converx account for this?

**What to Look For**:
- **Estimation Patterns**: Do you consistently underestimate or overestimate?
- **Completion Patterns**: What types of tasks do you complete? What do you avoid?
- **Energy Patterns**: Are you more effective at certain times? On certain types of work?
- **Blocker Patterns**: What types of blockers do you hit repeatedly?

**Optimization**:
- Update ACTION_PLAN.md: Structure goals to match your patterns
- Adjust expectations: Use your actual velocity, not aspirational velocity
- Create personal heuristics: "When Converx says X, I should interpret it as Y"

**Success Criteria**: You know your patterns and Converx helps you work with them, not against them

## Month 4-6: Mastery (Strategic Thinking)

### Month 4: Scenario Thinking

**Goal**: Use scenario bands for strategic decisions (Phase 1 feature)

**Actions**:
1. **Scenario Interpretation**: Understand optimistic/likely/conservative bands

3. **Risk Assessment**: What scenario are you tracking toward? Why?

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

### Pitfall 1: Expecting Perfection Immediately

**Problem**: Converx doesn't know you yet. Early recommendations may be off.

**Solution**: Give it time. Track accuracy. Calibrate expectations. The system learns.

### Pitfall 2: Ignoring Recommendations

**Problem**: If you never follow recommendations, Converx can't learn what works for you.

**Solution**: Follow recommendations when they make sense. Track outcomes. Provide feedback.

### Pitfall 3: Over-Optimizing Setup

**Problem**: Spending weeks setting up before using defeats the purpose.

**Solution**: Start simple. Use MVP features. Add complexity only when value is proven.

### Pitfall 4: Not Updating ACTION_PLAN.md

**Problem**: Stale goals lead to irrelevant recommendations.

**Solution**: Keep ACTION_PLAN.md current. Update goal status. Remove completed goals.

### Pitfall 5: Treating It Like a Task Manager

**Problem**: Converx is strategic, not tactical. It's not a to-do list.

**Solution**: Use it for "what should I do next?" not "what are all my tasks?"
