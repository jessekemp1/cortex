We stand at an inflection point. Artificial intelligence has given us **infinite execution leverage** - a single human can now orchestrate systems that would have required armies of specialists just a decade ago. But this gift has revealed a deeper crisis:

**The bottleneck is no longer execution. It is decision quality.**

Every day, you face thousands of micro-decisions:
- Which project deserves your focus this week?
- Should you push harder or protect your energy?
- What's the real cost of saying "yes" to this opportunity?
- How does this choice ripple across your health, finances, and relationships?

Traditional tools offer tasks, to-dos, and reactive assistance. They help you *do* things, but they don't help you *decide* what matters. They optimize for efficiency, not for alignment with your deeper purpose.

**This is the gap Converx fills.**

## The Life Weather Map: Seeing Your System as a Whole

Traditional productivity tools fragment your life into silos: work tasks, health goals, financial targets. But reality is interconnected. A 60-hour work week doesn't just affect your code - it impacts your sleep, your relationships, your runway, your long-term trajectory.

Converx models your life as a **Life Weather Map** - a structured view across domains (Work/Code, Finance, Health/Energy, Learning, Relationships) that shows:

- **State**: Key variables (workload, cognitive load, runway, volatility, sleep quality, stress)
- **Weather**: Qualitative summary (calm, high pressure, storm risk)
- **Horizons**: Nowcast (24-72h), short-term (1-4 weeks), long-term (quarters/years)

This is the substrate the strategist reasons over. It enables Converx to answer questions like:
- "Can I ship this project in 3 weeks without burning out?"
- "What's the real cost of taking on this new opportunity?"
- "How does my current trajectory look across all domains?"

**You cannot optimize what you cannot see. Converx makes the invisible visible.**

## Routes, Not Tasks: Strategic Navigation

Converx doesn't give you a task list. It gives you **routes** - ordered sequences of waypoints toward goals, each with:
- Entry conditions (what needs to be true to start)
- Exit conditions (what makes it complete)
- Risk notes and cross-domain dependencies

Routes are evaluated in **scenario bands**:
- **Optimistic**: Best reasonable case
- **Most likely**: Central trajectory
- **Conservative**: Safe, robust path

This is ensemble forecasting applied to life strategy - the same approach that makes weather prediction reliable, now applied to personal navigation.

**You navigate by routes, not by tasks. Converx is your navigation system.**

## Semi-Autonomous, Policy-Driven

Converx can not only advise but also **execute** - running bounded playbooks (tests, analyses, simulations, data pulls) under explicit policies:

- **Advisor mode**: Propose only; everything is manual

This is not about replacing human agency. It is about **extending agency** - freeing you from tactical execution so you can focus on strategic decisions.
