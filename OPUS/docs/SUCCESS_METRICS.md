# Converx Success Metrics

**Version**: 1.0  
**Date**: January 2025

---

## Overview

This document defines success metrics for Converx at three levels: Tactical (daily/weekly), Strategic (monthly/quarterly), and Existential (yearly). Each metric includes definition, measurement method, and target benchmarks.

**Key Principle**: What gets measured gets improved. Track metrics that matter, not metrics that are easy.

---

## Tactical Metrics (Daily/Weekly)

### Time Saved on Decision-Making

**Definition**: Time saved by using Converx vs manual decision-making

**Measurement**:
- **Before**: Time spent deciding what to work on (manual)
- **After**: Time to run `converx next` and pick action
- **Savings**: Before - After

**Target**: 30-60 minutes/day

**Tracking Method**:
- Simple: Estimate time saved daily
- Advanced: Track time spent on decision-making before/after

**Success Criteria**: 30+ minutes/day saved

---

### Recommendation Accuracy

**Definition**: How often Converx recommendations are useful/actionable

**Measurement**:
- **Total Recommendations**: Number of recommendations received
- **Useful Recommendations**: Number you followed or found valuable
- **Accuracy**: Useful / Total

**Target**: 70%+ useful recommendations

**Tracking Method**:
- Simple: Note which recommendations you followed
- Advanced: Track in spreadsheet with notes

**Success Criteria**: 70%+ recommendations are useful

---

### Effort Estimate Accuracy

**Definition**: How accurate Converx's effort estimates are

**Measurement**:
- **Predicted Hours**: Converx's estimate
- **Actual Hours**: Time actually spent
- **Error**: |Predicted - Actual| / Actual
- **Accuracy**: 1 - Average Error

**Target**: 80%+ accuracy (within 20% error)

**Tracking Method**:
- Record predicted hours from recommendations
- Record actual hours spent
- Calculate error weekly
- Track accuracy over time

**Success Criteria**: 80%+ accuracy (improving over time)

---

### Goal Completion Rate

**Definition**: Percentage of goals you complete

**Measurement**:
- **Total Goals**: Goals in ACTION_PLAN.md
- **Completed Goals**: Goals marked completed
- **Completion Rate**: Completed / Total

**Target**: 70%+ completion rate

**Tracking Method**:
- Review ACTION_PLAN.md weekly
- Count completed vs total goals
- Track completion rate over time

**Success Criteria**: 70%+ completion rate (up from baseline)

---

### Blocker Resolution Time

**Definition**: Time from blocker identification to resolution

**Measurement**:
- **Blocker Identified**: When Converx or you identify blocker
- **Blocker Resolved**: When blocker is cleared
- **Resolution Time**: Resolved - Identified

**Target**: 50% faster resolution

**Tracking Method**:
- Record when blockers are identified
- Record when blockers are resolved
- Calculate resolution time
- Compare to baseline (before Converx)

**Success Criteria**: 50% faster blocker resolution

---

## Strategic Metrics (Monthly/Quarterly)

### Decision Quality Improvement

**Definition**: Improvement in decision quality over time

**Measurement**:
- **Decision Outcomes**: Did decisions lead to desired results?
- **Decision Speed**: Time to make decisions
- **Decision Confidence**: How confident were you in decisions?
- **Quality Score**: Composite of outcomes, speed, confidence

**Target**: 25%+ improvement in quality score

**Tracking Method**:
- Record major decisions monthly
- Track outcomes quarterly
- Rate decision quality (1-10 scale)
- Compare to baseline

**Success Criteria**: 25%+ improvement in decision quality

---

### Prediction Accuracy (Strategic)

**Definition**: Accuracy of strategic predictions (scenarios, outcomes)

**Measurement**:
- **Predictions Made**: Scenario bands, outcome predictions
- **Actual Outcomes**: What actually happened
- **Accuracy**: How close predictions were to actuals

**Target**: 80%+ accuracy for strategic predictions

**Tracking Method**:
- Record predictions (scenarios, outcomes)
- Record actuals when available
- Calculate accuracy quarterly
- Track improvement over time

**Success Criteria**: 80%+ accuracy for strategic predictions

---

### Route Efficiency

**Definition**: How efficiently routes are completed (actual vs estimated)

**Measurement**:
- **Estimated Time**: Route estimate (from scenario bands)
- **Actual Time**: Time to complete route
- **Efficiency**: Estimated / Actual (1.0 = perfect, >1.0 = faster, <1.0 = slower)

**Target**: 0.9-1.1 efficiency (within 10%)

**Tracking Method**:
- Record route estimates
- Record actual completion times
- Calculate efficiency
- Track over multiple routes

**Success Criteria**: Routes completed within 10% of estimates

---

### Cross-Domain Balance

**Definition**: Balance across life domains (work, health, finance, etc.)

**Measurement**:
- **Domain Health Scores**: 0-1 score per domain
- **Balance Score**: Standard deviation of domain scores (lower = more balanced)
- **Target Balance**: All domains >0.6, std dev <0.15

**Target**: Balanced domains (all >0.6, low variance)

**Tracking Method**:
- Rate domain health monthly (0-1 scale)
- Calculate balance score
- Track trends over time

**Success Criteria**: All domains >0.6, improving balance

---

## Existential Metrics (Yearly)

### Capability Growth

**Definition**: Increase in personal capability over time

**Measurement**:
- **Output Quality**: Quality of work produced
- **Output Quantity**: Amount of work completed
- **Strategic Thinking**: Ability to think strategically
- **Pattern Recognition**: Ability to recognize patterns
- **Capability Score**: Composite of above

**Target**: 50%+ capability growth per year

**Tracking Method**:
- Annual self-assessment
- Compare to previous year
- Rate capability on 1-10 scale
- Track trends

**Success Criteria**: 50%+ capability growth per year

---

### Freedom Increase

**Definition**: Increase in options, runway, and flexibility

**Measurement**:
- **Financial Runway**: Months of runway
- **Time Freedom**: Hours available for strategic work
- **Option Count**: Number of viable options available
- **Freedom Score**: Composite of above

**Target**: 25%+ freedom increase per year

**Tracking Method**:
- Annual assessment of freedom metrics
- Compare to previous year
- Track trends

**Success Criteria**: 25%+ freedom increase per year

---

### Wisdom Accumulation

**Definition**: Accumulation of learned patterns and heuristics

**Measurement**:
- **Pattern Library**: Number of patterns identified
- **Heuristic Count**: Number of personal heuristics
- **Prediction Accuracy**: Improvement in prediction accuracy
- **Decision Quality**: Improvement in decision quality
- **Wisdom Score**: Composite of above

**Target**: Measurable wisdom accumulation

**Tracking Method**:
- Annual review of patterns and heuristics
- Track prediction and decision quality improvements
- Document wisdom gained

**Success Criteria**: Measurable wisdom accumulation

---

### Sustainable Pace

**Definition**: Ability to maintain high performance without burnout

**Measurement**:
- **Burnout Incidents**: Number of burnout episodes
- **Energy Baseline**: Average energy level
- **Recovery Time**: Time to recover from intense periods
- **Sustainability Score**: Composite of above

**Target**: Zero burnout, stable energy, fast recovery

**Tracking Method**:
- Track burnout incidents
- Monitor energy levels
- Measure recovery times
- Compare to baseline

**Success Criteria**: Zero burnout, stable energy, sustainable pace

## Measurement Methods

### Simple Tracking

**For Individual Users**:
- Note file with daily/weekly metrics
- Simple spreadsheet for calculations
- Weekly review to track trends

**Example**:
```
Week 1:
- Time saved: 35 min/day
- Recommendation accuracy: 65%
- Effort estimate accuracy: 75%

Week 2:
- Time saved: 42 min/day
- Recommendation accuracy: 72%
- Effort estimate accuracy: 78%
```

---

### Advanced Tracking

**For Power Users**:
- Automated data collection (scripts)
- Database for historical data
- Analytics dashboard
- Statistical analysis

**Tools**:
- Spreadsheet with formulas
- Python scripts for analysis
- Database for storage
- Visualization for trends
